03 · Multi-product practice

Systems for consistent products

Creating reusable patterns and shared design language across marketplace, SaaS, fintech, and client product work.

Role
Product & UX design
Focus
Patterns, components, guidance
Period
2019—Present
Tools
Figma · Ant Design · HTML/CSS
System / Scale

Context

A design system works when it makes the next decision easier.

Across Daraz, NETSOL, InvoZone, and Contegris, I worked on products with different audiences and maturity levels—from large marketplace platforms to new SaaS and internal tools.

The recurring problem

Teams lose time when similar interactions behave differently, component usage is unclear, or design decisions live only inside individual files. The visual inconsistency is only the visible symptom; the deeper cost is repeated discussion and uncertain implementation.

My contribution

  • Identified repeated interface needs across journeys and product states.
  • Created reusable UI patterns for forms, dashboards, navigation, feedback, and growth experiences.
  • Documented component usage and interaction guidance for product and engineering teams.
  • Applied accessibility-minded decisions to states, hierarchy, and feedback.
  • Used established systems such as Ant Design where they supported speed and consistency.

Outcome

25% improvement in design-system efficiency at Daraz.

Reusable patterns reduced repeated design effort and helped teams align faster. The same systems mindset now informs my enterprise work: solve the immediate flow while leaving behind a stronger foundation.
