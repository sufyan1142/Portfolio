Case Study

Deal-flow redesign case study

A portfolio-ready story about clarifying a deal workflow through mobile screens, progress states, comparison patterns, and sharper UX writing.

Role
UX narrative, screen review, case-study writing
Tools
Figma, notes, STAR framework
Status
Draft case study, needs final metrics

Problem

The work needed to explain a deal-flow experience clearly: what users were doing, where progress or comparison mattered, and how the interface helped people make decisions.

Process

  • Reviewed the visible product flow and noted the main interaction patterns.
  • Separated verified details from assumptions so the story would stay honest.
  • Reframed the work using Situation, Task, Action, and Result.
  • Prepared a concise version that could work for a portfolio or interview answer.

Outcome

The result is a cleaner case-study draft with a project overview, process narrative, interview talking points, and space for final screenshots, timeline, role details, and measurable results.
