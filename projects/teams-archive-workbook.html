Case Study

Teams self-chat archive workbook

A structured archive that turns a noisy Teams conversation into a readable workbook with summaries, categorized links, other messages, and raw material.

Role
Extraction planning, organization, workbook structure
Tools
Teams export, spreadsheet workflow, verification checks
Status
Completed archive pattern

Problem

The source material was useful but hard to scan: messages, links, timestamps, and loose context were mixed together inside a chat interface.

Process

  • Focused the archive on the requested self-chat rather than a raw dump.
  • Deduplicated messages and preserved raw material for auditability.
  • Separated summary, categorized links, other messages, and raw messages into different tabs.
  • Checked the workbook output so the artifact was usable, not merely exported.

Outcome

The final structure made the chat easier to review, search, and reuse. It also became a reusable pattern for future archives where organization matters more than volume.
